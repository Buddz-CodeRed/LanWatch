LanWatch MrK1 (version 0.01)
Home network monitoring system
=============================================================================

LanWatch is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. It monitors the
network it is installed on, and gives advice only: it never changes the
settings of any device on your network and never tries passwords.

Only scan networks you own or have permission to scan.

LanWatch runs entirely on this computer. Its web pages are served at
http://127.0.0.1:5000 and can only be opened from this computer. Nothing is
sent to the internet. The AI assistant runs locally.

During installation, if Nmap is not already installed, the official Nmap
installer (nmap-7.993-setup.exe) is downloaded from https://nmap.org and run.
Nmap and Npcap are separate products with their own licences, shown by their
own installer:
  - Nmap:  Nmap Public Source License (https://nmap.org/npsl/)
  - Npcap: Npcap License (https://npcap.com/oem/redist.html)
